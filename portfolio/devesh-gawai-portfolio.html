01 — About

Still figuring it out, on purpose

I'm currently studying Computer Science and Engineering at IET DAVV, Indore, after completing my first year of B.Tech at SGSITS Indore. Analytical problem-solving is what pulled me toward CSE, and I've spent the past year deepening that through software development and data-driven systems.

That interest didn't start in college. It goes back to Sacred Heart Co-Ed School, and it sharpened early when I attended a workshop at IIT Indore — my first real look at engineering and applied research in practice.

Outside the classroom, I play chess and spend time outdoors. Both feed the same instinct: read the situation, weigh the options, commit to a plan, and adjust when it doesn't go as expected.

The one-year detour at SGSITS is the part of my story I'd point to first — it's where I learned what it actually costs a student when nobody is coordinating the handoff.

06 — Why this role

Why I want to coordinate, not just attend

Four honest reasons, not a slogan.

I've been the student who needed a coordinator

Moving from SGSITS to IET DAVV mid-degree meant relearning a system from scratch. I know exactly what information a student is missing when nobody is actively bridging the gap.

I finish what I start

My projects — including one built around placement information itself — are small, but they're complete, documented, and public on GitHub. That's the same follow-through a coordinator role needs.

Chess trained the right instinct

Coordination is a lot like a mid-game position: multiple things moving at once, limited time, and a need to prioritize calmly instead of reacting to whatever is loudest.

I'm comfortable with data and tools

Between my CSE coursework and data analysis training, I'm at ease keeping information organized, current, and easy for the rest of the batch to actually use.
